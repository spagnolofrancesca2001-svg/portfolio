Works About

I'm a visual designer based in Bassano del Grappa and open to relocating. Since 2024 I've been working in-house at forte_forte, where I art direct campaigns and shoots and design most of what the brand puts out online, from newsletters to social. Before that I worked on brand identities at Numbered Studio in Amsterdam and Daam Studio in Thiene, after studying at IUAV in Venice and spending a year in Lisbon.

I mostly work with fashion, beauty and food brands. The briefs I like best are the ones where the obvious answer is already taken, and the projects I enjoy most are the ones I can follow all the way, from the logo to the set to the last story template.
